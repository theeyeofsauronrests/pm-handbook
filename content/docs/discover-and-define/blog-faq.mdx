---
title: Blog / FAQ
description: A lightweight artifact for aligning on a feature, program, project, or contract.
---

A Blog / FAQ is a feature- or program-level framing artifact.

Use it to create shared understanding before work fragments into stories, design tasks, technical spikes, and implementation details.

It borrows the useful part of working backwards: write the future explanation before the team falls in love with the implementation.

It is useful when:

- Defining a net-new feature
- Launching a program or project
- Starting work for a new customer or contract
- Coordinating across teams
- Clarifying intent before significant investment

It is not a contract, a complete PRD, or a replacement for tickets.

## Structure

### What are we building?

Describe the intended capability in plain language.

### What problem does it solve?

Describe the user, mission, or organizational problem.

### How are we solving it?

Explain the proposed approach, constraints, and important tradeoffs.

### User FAQ

Capture questions users, customers, or testers are likely to ask.

### Internal FAQ

Capture questions Product, Design, Engineering, security, delivery, and sustainment teams need answered.

## Relationship to tickets

The Blog / FAQ defines intent and direction.

Tickets coordinate execution.

If every ticket reopens the basic feature debate, the feature framing is probably incomplete.
